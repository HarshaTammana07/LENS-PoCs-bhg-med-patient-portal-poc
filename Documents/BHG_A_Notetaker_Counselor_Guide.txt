01  Introduction & What This Tool Does

Welcome to the BHG AI Notetaker
The BHG AI Notetaker is designed to help you stay fully present with your patients. Instead of splitting your attention between the conversation and your notes, the tool quietly listens, transcribes, and surfaces relevant insights in real time so you can focus on the person in front of you. When the session ends, it generates a structured DAP/PIE note draft that you review, edit, and then sync to SAMMS.
Think of it as a clinical assistant that handles documentation logistics but every word in the final record is yours to review, refine, and approve before it becomes part of the medical record.
How the AI Assists You
•	Transcribes the session in real time
•	Surfaces live AI Insights - safety flags, CBT opportunities, and engagement check items, during the session
•	Generates a structured DAP/PIE note draft after you end the session and review the transcript
•	Tracks whether key items (UDS results, location confirmation) have been addressed
•	Automatically saves clinician’s edits and logs every action in the audit trail

	The AI works for you ,not the other way around. You are always in control of what goes into the clinical record.
What the AI Will Never Do
The AI is designed with clear boundaries to protect both patients and clinicians:
What the AI Will NOT Do	Why This Matters
Diagnose a patient with any condition	Diagnosis requires clinical evaluation that is clinician’s responsibility
State or confirm suicidal/homicidal ideation as fact	SI/HI must be assessed and documented by the clinician
Invent observations, mood states, or assessments	All clinical content must be grounded in the actual session
Present AI suggestions as clinical conclusions	Insights are prompts, not diagnoses or treatment decisions
Sync notes without your explicit action	You are always the last step before anything enters SAMMS

Syncing a note to SAMMS is clinician’s clinical attestation. It is the equivalent of signing the note. Once synced, the note enters the medical record. By clicking Sync to SAMMS, clinicians are confirming that they have reviewed the AI-generated draft, made all necessary edits, and that the content accurately reflects the session and their clinical judgment.
	Syncing is clinician’s attestation. Synced notes cannot be edited again.

10  Compliance & Audit Guidelines

The BHG AI Notetaker is built to support your compliance obligations , not replace your professional responsibilities. Every key action in the tool is automatically timestamped in the audit trail.
What the Audit Trail Captures
The audit log records when actions occurred — not what the content was. This protects patient privacy while maintaining clinical accountability:
Audit Event	What Is Logged
Session start / end	Timestamp only
Note generated	Timestamp only
Note edited	Timestamp of when the note was changed — not what changed
Note regenerated	Timestamp of when regeneration occurred — not the previous content
Sync to SAMMS	Timestamp and success/failure status
Sync retry / failure	Timestamp and error status

	The audit trail records when actions happened, not the content of those actions. It is a timestamp log, not a version history of the note content.
Your Compliance Responsibilities
AI Disclosure
You must inform the patient at the start of each session that AI-assisted transcription and note generation is being used. The Engagement Check card tracks this as Addressed / Not Addressed.
Human Review Before Sync
Every note must be reviewed by you before it reaches SAMMS. Syncing without reading the note is a documentation liability.
Attestation via Sync
Clicking Sync to SAMMS serves as your clinical attestation. Ensure everything in the note is accurate and complete before syncing.
Safety Documentation
Any safety concern flagged by the AI — or arising in session — must be assessed by you and documented accurately. The AI flags possible signals; you confirm and record outcomes.
 Confidentiality
All session data, transcripts, and notes are protected under HIPAA. Do not share session content outside of authorised clinical channels.
 
11  AI Use Guidelines for Counselors

What AI Insights Are
AI Insights are real-time prompts generated from the live transcript. They surface things you might want to address , potential safety signals, therapeutic openings, or required clinical checklist items. They are always a starting point for your clinical thinking, never a conclusion.
What AI Insights Are Not
•	AI Insights are not diagnoses. A Safety card is a prompt to ask — not a clinical finding.
•	AI Insights are not mandatory. You decide whether and how to use each suggestion.
•	AI Insights are not always correct. The AI works from audio transcription, which can mishear or misattribute.
•	AI Insights are not a substitute for your therapeutic relationship and judgment.
Your Clinical Responsibilities — A Summary
Responsibility	What This Means
You conduct the session.	The AI listens and takes notes. You lead the clinical relationship.
You assess safety.	When a Safety card appears, you decide whether to address it, how, and what the outcome is.
You review every note.	The AI draft is a starting point. The final note is yours.
You sync when ready.	Syncing = attestation. Do not rush it.
You hold diagnostic responsibility.	The AI will never tell you what a patient has. That determination is always yours.

1. Executive Summary 

The BHG AI Counselor Note Taker is a web-based application designed to assist counselors at Behavioral Health Group (BHG) in documenting clinical sessions using AI-powered transcription and note generation. The application will integrate with BHG’s existing EMR system (SAMMS) and communication platform (Zoom Pro) to streamline the documentation workflow, reduce administrative burden on counselors, and improve the quality and consistency of clinical documentation across all 21 states where BHG operates treatment centers.  

The system captures session audio via an AI bot that joins Zoom Pro telehealth calls or through an in-browser recording feature for in-person sessions. It provides real-time AI-generated clinical nudges during sessions, automatically generates DAP or PIE formatted notes from session transcripts, and enables manual sync of finalized notes to SAMMS. Counselors retain full control over reviewing, editing, and approving notes before they are pushed to SAMMS, where supervisor co-signature and the standard EMR workflow continue as usual.  

 
2. Project Objectives 

Reduce counselor documentation time by automating clinical note generation from session audio and transcripts  

Improve clinical documentation quality and consistency through AI-assisted prompts, evidence-based practice nudges, and standardized note formatting  

Maintain strict HIPAA compliance and patient confidentiality throughout every step of the documentation workflow  

Integrate seamlessly with SAMMS (EMR) for pulling appointment/session data and pushing finalized notes  

Support multiple session types including in-person individual, telehealth individual (Zoom Pro), and unplanned walk-in sessions  

Provide admin-level analytics and performance metrics for clinical quality oversight and counselor performance tracking  

Support both DAP note format (used in 20 states) and PIE note format (used in North Carolina) based on treatment center configuration  

Enable counselors to focus on patient interaction during sessions rather than manual notetaking

6.7 SAMMS Integration and Sync  

SAMMS is BHG’s Electronic Medical Record (EMR) system and the system of record for all clinical documentation. The AI Note Taker integrates with SAMMS for both pulling session data and pushing finalized notes.  

6.7.1 Data Pull from SAMMS  

Counselor appointment/session schedules are pulled from the SAMMS scheduler  

Patient demographic information is pulled from SAMMS for session context  

Treatment center configuration (name, state, note template type) is maintained in sync with SAMMS  

Sync frequency and method to be determined based on SAMMS API capabilities  

6.7.2 Note Sync to SAMMS  

Sync is manual only — the counselor must explicitly click “Sync to SAMMS” for each approved note  

Before syncing, a confirmation dialog shows the note summary and asks the counselor to confirm  

The note data (D/A/P or P/I/E sections) is pushed to the corresponding fields in SAMMS  

Session metadata (date, duration, type, modality, patient, counselor) is included in the sync payload  

Upon successful sync, the note status in the AI application changes to “Synced”  

Once synced, the note is locked in the AI application — no further editing is allowed. SAMMS becomes the system of record.  

Any subsequent edits must be made directly in SAMMS by the counselor or supervisor  

Supervisor co-signature occurs within SAMMS, not within the AI application  

A sync history log is maintained showing: sync timestamp, counselor, note ID, success/failure status  

If sync fails (e.g., SAMMS unavailable), the counselor is notified and can retry

Responsive web design optimized for desktop (primary)

Wireframes and mockups to be shared with the clinical team for review and feedback before development  

Color is never used as the sole means of conveying information. Every status and tag uses both color and a label or icon
